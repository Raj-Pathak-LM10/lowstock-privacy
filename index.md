# Privacy Policy — Low Stock Alerts

*Last updated: 21 September 2026*

## What this app does

Low Stock Alerts monitors inventory levels in your Shopify store and emails you when products fall below a threshold you set.

## What we collect

When you install the app, we store:

- Your store's domain name
- The stock threshold you configure
- The email address you choose to receive alerts
- A record of which product variants have been included in recent alerts, including SKU, quantity, and timestamp

To generate alerts, the app reads your product and inventory data from Shopify. This data is not stored beyond what is listed above.

## What we do not collect

We do not access, store, or process any customer data. We do not read orders, customer records, payment information, or personal data belonging to your shoppers.

## How we use it

Solely to send you the low-stock alerts you have configured. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing.

## Where it lives

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase in the United States. The application runs on Fly.io in the United States. Alert emails are delivered through Resend. These providers process data only as necessary to operate the service.

## Retention and deletion

When you uninstall the app, all data associated with your store is deleted immediately. As an additional safeguard, we honour Shopify's shop data redaction request, which arrives approximately 48 hours after uninstall.

## Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of your data at any time by emailing [lowstockalerts.support@gmail.com]. Uninstalling the app achieves the same result immediately.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected in the date above.
